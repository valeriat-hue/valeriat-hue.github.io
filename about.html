Galaxy

Project 5050 Exhibition

Over the course of seven months, students and teachers from Freiberg and Dnipro have worked together in a Collaborative Online International Learning (COIL) project exploring the historical connection between their universities through the life and work of Joseph Tanatar, a geoscientist from Dnipro who studied in Freiberg at the beginning of the 20th century. Their commitment and collaborative work are now being showcased in this exhibition. To round off the project in style, the students presented their findings on Tanatar and the earth sciences at the turn of the century in five display cases they designed themselves, located in the entrance area of terra mineralia. Discover the students' work, stories and perspectives through this online exhibition, and join us celebrating not only the results of this project, but our longstanding and successful university partnership.
